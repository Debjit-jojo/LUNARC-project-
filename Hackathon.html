🚨 Emergency Medical Help


If you or someone around you has a life-threatening medical problem, seek emergency medical care immediately.


Emergency Warning Signs


  • Severe chest pain or pressure
  • Severe difficulty breathing
  • Loss of consciousness
  • Severe bleeding
  • Sudden weakness or paralysis
  • Severe confusion
  • Seizure
  • Severe allergic reaction
  • Suspected stroke
  • Major accident or trauma

📚 Health Information

❤️ Heart Health

Maintain physical activity, eat a balanced diet, avoid smoking, manage stress and monitor blood pressure where appropriate.

🫁 Respiratory Health

Persistent cough, breathing difficulty or chest symptoms should be assessed by a healthcare professional.

🧠 Mental Health

Stress, anxiety and sleep problems can affect daily life. Professional support can be useful when symptoms persist or become severe.

🍎 Nutrition

A balanced diet containing vegetables, fruits, whole grains, protein and adequate fluids supports general health.

💧 Hydration

Drink fluids regularly and pay attention to signs of dehydration, especially during illness or hot weather.

😴 Sleep

Regular sleep schedules and good sleep habits can support physical and mental wellbeing.
